PRIVACY POLICY FOR EMUCENTER

INTRODUCTION
This Privacy Policy describes how EmuCenter ("we", "our", or "us") handles information in connection with your use of our 
open-source emulator frontend application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding any 
data interaction.

INFORMATION COLLECTION AND STORAGE
EmuCenter operates primarily as a local application. We do not collect, store, or transmit personal user data to centralized servers.

* Local Configuration: User preferences, UI configurations, emulator paths, and ROM directory links are stored locally on your 
machine using standard configuration files (such as QSettings).
* Local Library: Game library metadata, custom artwork selections, and themes are managed locally on your device.

EXTERNAL INTEGRATIONS AND API USAGE
EmuCenter includes optional features that interact with third-party services. When you use these features, data is exchanged 
directly between your application and the respective service provider:

* IGDB Integration: When fetching game metadata and artwork, search queries are sent to the IGDB database to retrieve relevant 
game information.
* Discord Rich Presence (RPC): When enabled, the application communicates locally with your Discord desktop client to display 
your active game status, title, and platform according to native app-side integration standards.

DATA SECURITY
Because EmuCenter stores data locally on your device, you maintain full control over your files and settings. We do not maintain 
user accounts, passwords, or personal profiles.

CHILDREN'S PRIVACY
Our application does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. As a local software frontend, no age verification
or personal data collection mechanisms are implemented.

CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within the official repository, and your continued 
use of the application constitutes acceptance of those changes.

CONTACT
If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out through our official GitHub repository.
